Quest 3 AR Corner-Snapping Guide:
1. Point controller at real floor/corner (Green Reticle appears).
2. Pull Right Trigger to snap & lock entrance.
3. Right Thumbstick Left/Right: Rotate model.
4. Right Thumbstick Up/Down: Scale model.
5. Left Controller Grip Squeeze: Unlock & Move.
